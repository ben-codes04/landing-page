Everything you need to know about PulseTrack Premium

Can I use PulseTrack for free? +

Yes! Our Free plan includes up to 3 habits and basic cross-platform syncing, completely free forever with no credit card required.

What happens after I purchase Life-time access? +

You get unlimited access to all current and future Premium features across iOS, Android, macOS, and Web with a single, one-time payment.

Can I switch between Annual and Life-time plans? +

Absolutely. If you are on an Annual subscription, you can upgrade to the Life-time plan at any time through your account settings.

Do you offer refunds? +

We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee for all Premium purchases if you're not completely satisfied with PulseTrack.
